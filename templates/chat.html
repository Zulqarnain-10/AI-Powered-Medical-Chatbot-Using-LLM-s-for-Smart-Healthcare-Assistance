Live assistant · RAG over a medical reference

A medical assistant that cites its homework.

Ask about a condition, a medicine, or a diagnosis. The answer is not free-form generation: MedBot retrieves the most relevant passages from a medical reference text and the model is constrained to them — when the reference does not support an answer, it says it does not know.

For information only. MedBot is a portfolio demonstration, not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If this is an emergency, contact local emergency services.

How it works

Grounded, not guessing.

A retrieval-augmented generation pipeline. The model never answers from memory alone — the reference is retrieved first, every time.

  1. 01

    Ingest & chunk

    The medical reference PDF is split into overlapping 500-character chunks, so each passage stays small enough to retrieve precisely.

  2. 02

    Embed

    Every chunk becomes a 384-dimension vector with the open all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence-transformer.

  3. 03

    Retrieve

    Your question is embedded the same way, and Pinecone returns the three most similar passages by cosine similarity.

  4. 04

    Answer

    An OpenAI model writes a concise answer constrained to those passages — and declines when they do not support one.
